1. What a Membership Is

1.1 A Daily Adventure Box Membership is a paid, subscription-style plan that gives you a set number of included rental hours ("Included Hours") each billing cycle at a fixed monthly price, instead of paying the standard per-rental rate every time you use a Locker.

1.2 DAB currently offers multiple Membership tiers (for example, "Bronze" and "Gold"), which differ by monthly price, number of Included Hours, and — for some tiers — the categories of equipment those hours may be used on. The specific tiers, prices, Included Hours, and any category restrictions available to you are shown at the time you enroll and in your account dashboard.

1.3 A Membership is not a rental itself. It is a billing plan that determines how your rentals are priced. Every individual rental you make as a member remains subject to the General Terms.

1.4 A Membership renews automatically and continuously on a recurring billing cycle (typically monthly) until you cancel it as described in Section 4, or until we suspend or terminate it as permitted under these Membership Terms or the General Terms.

2. Billing and Auto-Renewal

2.1 Automatic Charges. When you enroll in a Membership, you authorize DAB (through our payment processor, Stripe) to automatically charge your payment method the applicable Membership price at the start of each billing cycle, without further action or confirmation from you, until you cancel.

2.2 Billing Cycle. Your billing cycle begins on the date you first enroll and repeats on that same date each subsequent cycle (for example, if you enroll on the 9th, you will be billed on the 9th of each following cycle, adjusted as needed for shorter months). You can view your next billing date and payment history in your account at any time.

2.3 Payment Method on File. You must keep a valid, current payment method on file with Stripe. If a charge fails, we may retry it, suspend Included Hours access until payment succeeds, and/or treat your Membership as cancelled if payment is not resolved within a reasonable period we will communicate to you.

2.4 Overage and Non-Member Rentals. Rental time beyond your Included Hours in a given cycle, and any rentals falling outside a category your tier covers, are billed separately at DAB's then-current standard rates, in accordance with the General Terms. Overage charges may be billed to the same payment method on file.

2.5 Price Changes. We may change Membership prices for future billing cycles. If we do, we will give you advance notice of at least thirty (30) days before the new price takes effect, by email and/or in-app notice to the address/account on file. If you do not cancel before your next billing date after that notice, your next charge will reflect the new price. Price changes never apply retroactively to a cycle you already paid for.

2.6 Taxes. Membership prices may not include applicable sales, use, or similar taxes, which will be added to your charge where required by law.

3. Included Hours: How They Work and Reset Each Cycle

3.1 No Rollover. Included Hours are use-it-or-lose-it. Any Included Hours you do not use by the end of a billing cycle do not carry over to the next cycle, do not accumulate, and are not refunded, credited, or banked in any way. At the start of each new billing cycle, your Included Hours balance simply resets to the full amount for your tier.

3.2 How Included Hours Are Applied. When you start a rental as a member, DAB will automatically apply your Included Hours to that rental first, before any overage billing kicks in, up to the number of hours remaining in your current cycle.

3.3 Category-Restricted Tiers. If your tier restricts Included Hours to a specific equipment category (for example, a tier that only covers paddleboards, or only covers bikes), your Included Hours will only apply to rentals within that category. Rentals of equipment outside your tier's category are billed at standard non-member/overage rates and do not draw down your Included Hours balance.

3.4 Partial-Hour Rentals. If a rental uses only part of an hour, we round and deduct from your Included Hours balance to the nearest fifteen (15) minutes, as shown at checkout and in your account. Once your Included Hours balance for the cycle reaches zero, additional rental time is billed at standard overage rates for the remainder of that cycle.

4. Cancellation

4.1 How to Cancel. You may cancel your Membership at any time through your account settings in the app, or by contacting us at support@dailyadventurebox.com. Cancellation is effective as described below — it does not require any minimum notice period.

4.2 What Cancellation Does. Cancelling stops future auto-renewal charges. It does not end your Membership immediately. Instead:

  • You keep full Membership access — including any remaining Included Hours for your current cycle — through the end of the billing period you already paid for.
  • Your Membership then simply does not renew, and you will not be charged again.
  • We do not provide prorated refunds for the unused portion of a cycle you cancel partway through, because you retain full access through the end of that cycle.

4.3 Re-Enrolling. You may re-enroll in a Membership at any time after cancellation, subject to whatever tiers and pricing are then available. Re-enrolling starts a new billing cycle and a fresh Included Hours balance; it does not restore any hours or time from a prior, expired cycle.

5. Changing Tiers (Upgrades and Downgrades)

5.1 Making a Change. You can request to upgrade or downgrade your Membership tier at any time through your account.

5.2 When It Takes Effect. Tier changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle, not immediately. You keep your current tier's price, Included Hours, and category restrictions (if any) for the remainder of the cycle you're currently in.

5.3 Billing Impact. Your first charge at the new tier's price will occur at your next regular billing date. Changing tiers does not trigger an immediate additional charge or refund for the cycle already in progress.

5.4 Included Hours on a Tier Change. Because Included Hours reset each cycle and do not roll over (Section 3.1), your Included Hours balance under the new tier starts fresh at the beginning of the new cycle — it is not combined with, or reduced by, unused hours from your prior tier.
