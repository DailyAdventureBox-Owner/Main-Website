Questions?

Frequently Asked

What types of brands work best with DAB?

Outdoor recreation brands, sunscreen, surf/SUP brands, hydration products, sports gear, local businesses (restaurants, bars, hotels), fitness brands, and adventure tourism companies.

How long are advertising commitments?

Minimum 3 months recommended to see impact, but we offer month-to-month flexibility. Seasonal packages are also available for summer campaigns.

Can I put my own products inside the lockers?

Yes. Full Partnership includes product placement inside the lockers. Renters can try your gear firsthand — the best advertising is experience.

Where are the lockers located?

Currently at Camp Lejeune, NC — including Onslow Beach, Turtle Beach, and Hospital Point. Expanding to more bases and beaches nationwide in 2026-2027.

Do you provide analytics?

Yes. All packages include monthly reports with QR code scans, app impressions, social media reach, and engagement metrics.

Who designs the locker wraps?

Our team handles design and production. You provide your brand assets and we create the wrap. You approve everything before it goes on the locker.
