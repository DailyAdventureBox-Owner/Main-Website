Questions?

Frequently Asked

What types of brands work best with DAB?

Outdoor recreation brands, sunscreen, surf/SUP brands, hydration products, sports gear, local businesses (restaurants, bars, hotels), fitness brands, and adventure tourism companies.

When do I get billed?

Only during high-traffic months at your locker's location. For beach locations, that's May through September (5 months). Your ad stays on the locker year-round — off-season months are free visibility.

Can I put my own products inside the lockers?

Yes. Full Partnership includes product placement inside the lockers. Renters can try your gear firsthand — the best advertising is experience.

Where are the lockers located?

Currently at Camp Lejeune, NC — including Onslow Beach, Turtle Beach, and Hospital Point. Expanding to more bases and beaches nationwide in 2026-2027.

Do you provide analytics?

Yes. All packages include monthly reports with QR code scans, app impressions, social media reach, and engagement metrics.

Who designs the locker wraps and who pays for printing?

We handle everything — design, print, and installation — at no extra cost to you. Just send us your logo, brand colors, and any imagery. You approve the final design before it goes to print. We cover production because we want high-quality wraps on every locker.

What happens when my season ends?

Your wrap stays on the locker through the off-season at no charge — free brand visibility. When the next season starts, you get first right of renewal at the same rate if you commit before March 1.
