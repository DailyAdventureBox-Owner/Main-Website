Questions?

Frequently Asked

What if something breaks?

We handle all maintenance and equipment replacement. You never touch the locker — it's fully managed by DAB.

How big is the locker?

Standard setup is one large locker (surfboards/SUPs) + one V2 small gear locker. Total footprint is about 4' x 6'. Custom configurations available.

Do I need power or internet?

No. Lockers are solar-powered with cellular connectivity. They operate completely independently.

How much can I earn as a Sales Rep?

You earn a percentage of revenue from every location you refer — for as long as that locker is active. The more locations, the more you earn.
