Questions

FREQUENTLY ASKED

What type of gear is in the lockers? +−

Each locker is stocked with premium adventure and outdoor recreation gear — paddleboards, kayak gear, snorkel sets, beach chairs, water hammocks, recreation equipment, and more. We curate the inventory specifically for your location's environment and guest profile.

How much revenue will I earn? +−

There's no one-size-fits-all rate. We negotiate revenue share on a per-location basis based on your expected traffic, location type, and locker size. We'll present clear numbers before anything is signed — no surprises.

Do I need to do anything after it's installed? +−

No. Daily Adventure Box handles all restocking, gear maintenance, sanitization between rentals, and customer support. Your only responsibility is having a designated space for the locker.

What if gear gets damaged or stolen? +−

All equipment is covered under our commercial insurance policy. Renters agree to our terms of service at checkout. Any damage, loss, or theft is handled entirely by us — your property carries zero liability.

How much space does it take up? +−

Our smallest unit — the CLJ-07 V2 Standalone — is just 30" wide × 21" deep × 50.75" tall. That's a footprint smaller than a mini-fridge. It can be installed indoors or outdoors. We'll do a site walkthrough before installation and confirm the right placement for your space.

What does the contract look like? +−

We work out terms on a per-location basis. Our standard agreement covers installation, revenue split, maintenance responsibilities, insurance, and exit terms. It's straightforward and written to protect both parties.

Can I see a demo or visit an existing location? +−

Yes — contact us and we'll arrange a product walkthrough, connect you with an existing partner, or bring a unit directly to your property for a hands-on look.
