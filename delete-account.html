What Happens

WHAT GETS DELETED, WHAT STAYS

Deletion removes your account and personal profile right away. A small set of records is kept for a limited time because the law and our payment processor require it — exactly as described in our Privacy Policy.

Deleted when you confirm

  • −Your login and every way to sign in to it: email and password, Sign in with Apple, Sign in with Google, and any active sessions
  • −Your profile: name, date of birth, phone number, and the physical details used for gear sizing
  • −Payment methods saved to your profile, your membership and any membership waitlist spot, loyalty points, referral links and promo-code redemptions
  • −Your cart, support chat history, open problem reports, and parent or guardian consent details on file

Kept for a limited time, as described in our Privacy Policy

  • ✓Rental and transaction records (receipts, charges, deposits, refunds), held by us and by our payment processor, Stripe, for tax, accounting and fraud-prevention purposes, and no longer linked to a login
  • ✓Legal records such as signed rental agreements and waivers, for the retention period in the Privacy Policy
  • ✓Security and fraud-prevention logs for a short period, and anonymized analytics that cannot identify you

Retention periods and the legal basis for each are listed in the Privacy Policy. Once a period ends, the record is permanently deleted.

Before you start: deletion is blocked while you have an active rental or an unpaid balance. Return the gear or settle the balance in the app first — this page will tell you if that applies to your account.

    Other Ways

    SIGNED IN? DELETE FROM THE APP

    This page exists so you can delete your account without signing in. If you still have the app, the same deletion is one tap away.

    In the Daily Adventure Box app

    Open the app, go to Profile, and choose Delete account. You'll be asked to confirm by typing DELETE. It removes exactly the same data as this page.

    Lost access to your email?

    If you can no longer read the email address on your account, write to support@dailyadventurebox.com from any address and we'll verify it's you another way.
