Delete Your Account & Data
Daily Adventure Box, Inc.
Document ID: DAB-DA-2026-01 Version: 1.0 Effective: July 2026

You can request deletion of your Daily Adventure Box ("DAB") account and associated personal data at any time. This page explains how to submit a request, what happens to your data, and how long it takes.

1. How to Request Deletion

Email privacy@dailyadventurebox.com from the email address associated with your account and include the subject line "Delete My Account." If you no longer have access to that inbox, contact support@dailyadventurebox.com and we will help verify your identity another way.

2. Verification

To protect your data from unauthorized deletion, we verify that the request came from the account owner using the email of record plus a secondary factor (a code sent to the phone number on file, or a detail from a prior rental/transaction). No data is deleted until verification clears.

3. What Gets Deleted

  • Your account profile (name, phone, date of birth, address)
  • Login credentials and stored authentication tokens
  • Marketing preferences and communication opt-in/opt-out records (after the retention window below)
  • Geolocation and locker-unlock event history

4. What We Retain, and Why

Some records must be retained for a limited period even after account deletion, to meet tax, financial, and legal obligations:

DataRetained ForReason
Stripe transaction records & receipts7 yearsIRS recordkeeping requirements
Rental activity (aggregated)3 yearsDispute resolution & fraud prevention
Signed agreements / e-signature records7 yearsContract dispute statute of limitations
Security event logs90 days (email hashed after 30)Fraud & abuse prevention

Once the applicable retention window closes, this data is permanently and irreversibly deleted, including from backups (backups roll off within 30 days of the live record's deletion).

5. Timeline

  1. We acknowledge your request within 10 business days.
  2. We complete deletion within 45 calendar days of verification (extendable by 45 additional days for complex requests, with written notice explaining why).
  3. We send written confirmation of what was deleted and what was retained, along with the legal basis for any retention.
Deleting your account also cancels any active rentals or holds. If you have an open rental or an outstanding balance, resolve it before requesting deletion, or contact support first.

6. Questions

See our full Privacy Policy for more detail on how your data is collected, used, and protected. For anything not covered here, email support@dailyadventurebox.com.
