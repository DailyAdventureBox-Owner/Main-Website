Our Story

Built by a Marine. Built to Last.

DailyAdventureBox was founded by Shay Putnam, an active-duty U.S. Marine, with a simple idea: make outdoor adventure gear accessible to everyone — no hauling, no reservations, no hassle.

What started as a single locker at Pensacola Beach has grown into a smart locker platform expanding across military bases and beaches nationwide.

2023

The First Launch — First DAB locker deployed at Pensacola Beach, FL

2024

Rebuild — Complete system redesign: new app, new lockers, new platform

2025

Camp Lejeune — Partnered with MCCS. First lockers live at Onslow Beach.

2026

Expansion — 8 Camp Lejeune locations, Fund Adventure portal, national base expansion plan
