Our Story

Built by a Marine. Built to Last.

DailyAdventureBox was founded by Shay Putnam, a U.S. Marine Captain, with a simple idea: make outdoor adventure gear accessible to everyone — no hauling, no reservations, no hassle.

What started as a single locker at Pensacola Beach has grown into a smart locker platform expanding across military bases and beaches nationwide.

2023

The First Launch — First DAB locker deployed at Pensacola Beach, FL

2024

Rebuild — Complete system redesign: new app, new lockers, new platform

2025

Camp Lejeune — MCCS contract signed. Base credentialing and site prep underway.

2026

Expansion — 8 Camp Lejeune locations live, national base expansion plan underway

2027

National Rollout — USMC installations nationwide via MCCS master contract. 200+ target locations.

The DAB Story

From One Locker
to a Growing Network.

A Marine Captain's idea — built in a garage, tested on a beach, contracted onto a military base, and now scaling nationwide.

2022

The Idea

Shay Putnam, an active-duty U.S. Marine Captain, keeps seeing the same problem at every base and beach: people want to get on the water but don't own gear, can't haul it, or can't afford to rent it the traditional way. He starts sketching a smart locker that lives at the beach and unlocks from your phone.

2023

First Locker. First Rentals.

The first DAB locker is built by hand and deployed at Pensacola Beach, FL. It rents surfboards, paddle boards, and beach gear to real customers. The model works — gear goes out, comes back, revenue flows. Patent application filed.

Proof of concept
2024

The Rebuild

Real-world deployment exposed gaps in hardware and software. The entire system is rebuilt from scratch — new locker chassis, new circuit boards, new app, new backend. Version 2 is stronger, smarter, and designed to scale. More Pensacola Beach locations added during the rebuild.

2025

Camp Lejeune Contract

DAB signs a contract with MCCS (Marine Corps Community Services) at Camp Lejeune, NC — the largest Marine base on the East Coast. Site selection, base credentialing, and installation prep begin across Onslow Beach, Hospital Point, Courthouse Bay, and New River Marina.

MCCS contract signed
2026

8 Locations Live — Camp Lejeune

All 8 Camp Lejeune locations go operational across four venues: Onslow Beach, Hospital Point Rec Area, Courthouse Bay Dock, and New River Marina. The first micro-owners come on board. BOTE gear — SUPs, kayaks, and water equipment — is loaded into the lockers.

● Live now
2027

National Rollout

Expanding to USMC installations nationwide via an MCCS master contract. Target: 200+ locations across military bases and beaches in NC, FL, VA, SC, HI, AZ, GA, CA, and beyond. Franchise and partner tiers open to outside operators.
